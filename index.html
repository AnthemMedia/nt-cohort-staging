The Model

Not a class.
Not a tutor.
A cohort.

We hand-select 2–6 students at similar skill levels, pair them with one of our elite tutors, and move them through a structured, high-accountability program together.

The result? Students push each other. They normalize struggle. They build confidence not just from the tutor — but from watching peers solve hard problems in real time.

It's the intensity of private tutoring with the motivational engine of a team.

Why the Cohort Works

Twenty-five years of tutoring taught us something counterintuitive: sometimes the best thing for a student isn't being alone with a tutor.

01

Curated, Not Random

Every cohort is hand-assembled by skill level, learning style, and goals. This isn't a sign-up-and-show-up class. It's a deliberately built team.

02

Productive Struggle, Together

Students watch peers work through the same hard problems they're facing. Struggle becomes normal. Confidence builds faster than it ever does alone.

03

Elite Tutor at the Helm

Every cohort is led by a top 1% tutor — the same caliber you'd get in a private session, now orchestrating a small team toward measurable breakthroughs.
